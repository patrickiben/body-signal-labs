The math behind it

Worked on 5-second steps of the ride. Signals that move together share credit in both directions here, so the first two percentages overlap. The net leader, the difference between them, is the part that says which one led.

Not computed from your ride

  • Manuscript 1 (CRT) needs breathing, EEG, skin conductance and beat-to-beat data across epochs. A TCX ride has heart rate and cadence only.
  • Heart-rate variability (DFA α1, RMSSD, SDNN, Lomb power, sample entropy) needs beat-to-beat intervals, which a TCX file doesn't store.
  • Breathing coupling needs a breathing signal, and nothing you connect measures breathing.
  • The JEPA pacing planner in Manuscript 2 runs on the ten-signal sample network, not on two signals.
  • Manuscript 3: its recurrence and readout curves are properties of the model and come out the same for any ride (tab 3).
