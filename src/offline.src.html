Your ride

Ride the Biofeedback Path Cockpit with a heart-rate strap connected, open Session, press Start recording, and when you finish press Export TCX. Then open that file here with "Open a ride (.tcx)". A TCX file from another app works too if it has heart rate and cadence. Nothing leaves this browser.

The math behind it

Worked on 5-second steps of the ride. Signals that move together share credit in both directions here, so the first two percentages overlap. The net leader, the difference between them, is the part that says which one led.
